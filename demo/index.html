BeliefView v2: local demo

Synthetic 2D simulation only. No real drone, camera, flight controller or real-world validation is involved, and nothing here predicts real drone performance.

An observer "drone" loses a moving subject behind cover. It keeps a probability map of where the subject might be, including a chance that it left the search area, updates that map when it looks and sees nothing, and greedily (not optimally) picks its next viewpoint. It is compared with simple search patterns on the same pre-rolled episodes.

What the picture shows

Heatmap
The filter's probability that the subject is in each cell, on the same fixed colour scale (0 to 0.05) in every frame. It is the unconditional probability and is never renormalised, so the map fades as probability moves to "outside".
Outside bar
P(outside): the filter's probability that the subject has left the 40 x 40 search area. Map plus bar always sum to 1.
Blue wedge and dot
The observer and its 60-degree, range-12 field of view. The wedge is brighter on steps that count as a new independent look.
Candidate viewpoints
Small circles: the places the greedy planner scored at its latest planning step (darker green = higher score = P(reacquire within 12 steps) minus lam x travel steps). The green star and line are the single best candidate and the path to it.
Evaluation-only truth
The orange diamond is the subject's true position. It is drawn for the viewer and for scoring only. The filter and planner never receive it: they get the observer's own pose, one noisy last-seen point and its age, and for each look a detect / no-detect flag (with a noisy position on a detection). The line under each clip marked [evaluation-only] is likewise not planner input.
Yellow star
A detection (the simulated sensor has no false positives, so a detection is always the subject).

4. The motion-mismatch failure

In scenario B the subject makes long stops and doubles back, while the filter still assumes short stops and rare reversals. There BeliefView reacquired 0.68 of subjects against 0.67 for Last-seen (difference +0.01 [-0.03, +0.05]; not distinguishable) while flying 31.2 units against 17.7: the belief machinery bought nothing measurable over the simple baseline here, at 1.8 times the travel.

Its deadline report was also unreliable under this mismatch: among unresolved scenario B runs whose subject was still inside, it wrongly declared "left the area" in 2 of 9, and it correctly declared "left" for only 16 of the 56 that had left. The failure clip above (B motion mismatch, episode 0) is a plain miss rather than a wrong declaration: the subject (true behaviour: continue) was still inside at the deadline, was never detected, and the final stated P(outside) was 0.30, so no "left" call was made. It was picked by the fixed rule (first such episode), not because it shows the wrong-declaration failure.

5. Limitations

  • Simulation only. One fixed 40 x 40 map of rectangles, an abstract 2D sensor model, hand-written subject behaviours, perfect self-pose, no altitude, camera projection, wind, latency or battery.
  • No false positives. The simulated sensor never reports a subject that is not there. Real detectors do.
  • Many true exits. 113 of 200 (A matched), 97 of 200 (B motion mismatch), 121 of 200 (C sensor mismatch), 140 of 200 (D exit mismatch) subjects leave the map before the deadline, which caps every method's reacquisition rate.
  • Small still-inside denominators. False-"left" rates and region coverage rest on 11, 9, 14, 4 still-inside unresolved runs (A, B, C, D): too few to estimate a rate.
  • Unadjusted intervals. Many comparisons are shown; some intervals would exclude 0 by chance alone.
  • Travel cost. BeliefView flies 1.8 to 2.2 times as far as Last-seen across the four scenarios (section 3).
  • Greedy planner. One-step choice among a small candidate set; not globally optimal.
  • v1 is an earlier record, not this result. v1 used lam = 0 (the travel penalty was inactive) and its 100 test episodes per scenario were run twice. It is kept unmodified in results/ and archive/v1/ and its tables are not merged with v2.
